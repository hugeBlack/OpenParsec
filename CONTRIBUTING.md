# Contributing

OpenParsec is maintained in our spare time, and we try to hold our own PRs to the same bar.

## Pull requests

Keep each PR to one fix or one feature. You will be asked to split your PR before review if it mixes unrelated changes.

A project that builds doesn't mean it passes testing. Run it in the simulator (aka Device Hub in Xcode 27) on iOS 26 or older, and preferably, against a real device.

Before you change application behavior, think about the experience from the user's side, not just the code.

## AI tools

You own what you ship.

Feel free to have agents autocomplete your code, we don't ask you to label it, but double and triple-check your commits. Write your PRs from what you actually tested, and if you can't explain your changes in a PR body in your own words, your PR won't be merged.

## Ideas and questions

Please post feature requests and questions in the repo's [Discussions](https://github.com/hugeBlack/OpenParsec/discussions) page, issues are for bugs.
